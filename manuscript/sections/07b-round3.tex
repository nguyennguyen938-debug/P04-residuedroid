\subsection{Natural races and label errors}
\label{sec:eval-round3}

\paragraph{Natural race.} Without a barrier, we uploaded realistic payloads (crash reports of a few hundred KiB, analytics
batches, multi-MiB backups) over throttled emulator links and withdrew at a uniformly random time during the upload.
Over \raceTrials{} trials per link and variant, layered early checks accepted bytes after acknowledgement in
\raceEdgeLayer{} (EDGE), \raceThreegLayer{} (3G) and \raceLteLayer{} (LTE) trials; the fence did so in \raceEdgeFence{},
\raceThreegFence{} and \raceLteFence{}. Equation~\ref{eq:race} predicts \racePredPct{}\% for the layered checks; we
observed \raceObsPct{}\%.

\paragraph{Label errors.} Each mutant removes or corrupts one label propagation point (artifact \texttt{mutation/}).
Keeping only the newest label of a summary leaked data in \mutOneLeak{} of \mutTrials{} trials; the test-time checker
flagged \mutOneFlagged{} of \mutOneWrong{} mislabelled items. Labelling raw records with the current epoch leaked in
\mutTwoLeak{} trials, with \mutTwoFlagged{} of \mutTwoWrong{} items flagged. Dropping the label from the job input
failed closed: no leak, and \mutThreeDeficit{} permitted items undelivered. Runtime enforcement cannot detect a valid
but incomplete label; a test-time comparison of labels with traced content can.

\paragraph{Newer Android and parameters.} On Android~14 (\lifeFourteenTrials{} paired trials), layered early and fence
had \lifeFourteenLayer{} and \lifeFourteenFence{} violating trials; on Android~15 (\lifeFifteenTrials{}),
\lifeFifteenLayer{} and \lifeFifteenFence{}. Median withdrawal acknowledgement ranged from \sweepAckSmallMs{}\,ms
(1\,KiB chunks, one sender) to \sweepAckLargeMs{}\,ms (64\,KiB, 16 senders); CPU from \sweepCpuSmall{} to
\sweepCpuLarge{}\,ms/MiB.

\paragraph{Physical device.} On \devModel{} (Android~\devAndroid{}), the TLS barrier repeated over \devTrials{} trials:
the early check completed \devEarlyComplete{} requests, and the fence accepted application ciphertext after
acknowledgement in \devFenceAfterAck{} trials.
