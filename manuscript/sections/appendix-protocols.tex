\paragraph{Earlier protocol versions.} Where a protocol was revised, the earlier runs are kept and their outcomes were as
follows. Natural race, emulator: the first two protocols failed their link-shaping gate in pilots, so no final trial ran.
Rented phones: the first TLS run matched the rerun in every case except one Galaxy case whose shorter deadline ended
requests early; in both runs no fenced case accepted bytes after the acknowledgement. Cost sweep: the first protocol's
probes mostly missed the send window, and the second lost trials to the test receiver's timeout; their medians are in the
artifact. Label mutants: in the first protocol the fixture never re-aggregated after the re-grant, so M1 could not
trigger; M2 leaked and M3 failed closed as in the second.
