\section{Conclusion}
Persisted jobs and copied data can outlive withdrawal. Durable source lineage and epochs make the policy decision meaningful across derivation and re-grant; a bounded-write fence closes a residual race after earlier checks. The matched lifecycle results do not establish a general advantage over layered checks with equivalent metadata. HTTP and TLS tests distinguish encryption, socket acceptance and receipt. Complete mediation and complete labels remain assumptions, and accepted bytes cannot be recalled. ACRA and Matomo embeddings demonstrate supported paths without establishing production-app or physical-device coverage.
