\section{Conclusion}
Persisted jobs and copied data can outlive withdrawal. Durable source lineage and epochs make the policy decision meaningful across derivation and re-grant; a bounded-write fence closes a residual race after earlier checks. In lifecycle schedules, layered checks with equivalent metadata show no confirmed disadvantage; when a withdrawal lands during an upload, on an emulator or a phone, a check placed before the request leaks and the fence does not. A real app embedding shows the same split, and a test-time label check catches the label errors that runtime enforcement cannot see. Complete mediation and complete labels remain assumptions, and accepted bytes cannot be recalled.
