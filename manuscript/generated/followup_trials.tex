\paragraph{Permitted-flow deficits.} Across all five variants, the 448 undelivered permitted items lie in 70 reboot tenant-trials. In each, JobScheduler stopped a running upload at shutdown (connectivity stop reason), the worker exited at a batch boundary, the fixture's stop callback declines rescheduling, and the app process did not restart before the observation window closed (median 57\,s after boot completion). No affected trace contains a policy block, a send failure, or a skip or cancellation of the stopped job. The variants differ only in how many permitted jobs were mid-upload at shutdown (layered early 29, job start 16, chunk fence 15, layered + fence 10, enqueue 0 of 160 reboot tenant-trials each). We attribute these deficits to the fixture's reboot handling, not to check placement.
