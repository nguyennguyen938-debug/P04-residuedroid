12   ABSTRACT                                                                                                                            65
13   Android apps persist outboxes and jobs that can outlive                                                                             66
14   a user’s withdrawal. The enforcement problem is not just                                                                            67
15   when to check a toggle: derived data must retain its source                                                                         68
16   policy, and old data must remain distinguishable after                                                                              69
17   consent is granted again. ResidueDroid combines durable                                                                             70
18   source epochs, labels copied with persisted data, and a                                                                             71
19   policy check serialized with each bounded socket write. We                                                                          72
20   separate collection-only opt-outs, restrictions and deletion                                                                        73
21   contracts, and evaluate them with ten controlled workloads.                                                                         74
22   A 360-app static screen identifies candidate deferred paths,                                                                        75
23   not runtime violations. The lifecycle experiments expose                                                                            76
24   distinct lineage, epoch and retry failures; their rates depend                                                                      77
25   on the fixture mix. Matched layered checks with the same                                                                            78
26   metadata show no confirmed disadvantage in the measured                                                                             79
27   lifecycle schedules. The late fence instead closes a narrow                                                                         80
28   residual race: in 20 HTTPS trials with withdrawal after                                                                             81
29   TLS buffering, an early check completes every request                                                                               82
30   whereas the fence accepts no application ciphertext after                                                                           83
31   acknowledgement. ACRA and Matomo embeddings exercise                                                                                84
32   persisted-report and queued-dispatcher paths. The guaran-                                                                           85
33   tee requires complete mediation and complete labels; a valid                                                                        86
34   but incomplete developer label bypasses it. Already accepted                                                                        87
35   bytes can arrive later, and emulator measurements do not                                                                            88
36   establish physical-device energy or deployment behavior.                                                                            89
